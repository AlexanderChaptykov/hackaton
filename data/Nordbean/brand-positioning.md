# Nordbean brand positioning

> Mock data. Nordbean is a fictional company.

Internal document. Owner: Head of Brand. Last updated: March 2026.

## Positioning statement

For people who care how their coffee tastes and where it comes from, Nordbean is the online roaster that delivers traceable specialty coffee within days of roasting, without tying anyone into a contract.

## Target audiences

| Audience | Who they are | What they want | Main products |
|----------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| Home enthusiasts | 28–45, urban, own a grinder or are about to buy one | Better coffee at home, new origins to try, no effort to reorder | P1, P2, P3 |
| Gift buyers | 25–55, buying for a partner, parent or colleague | A gift that looks thoughtful and needs no coffee knowledge | P1 |
| Office managers | Teams of 10–100, mostly agencies, tech and consulting firms | Coffee the team is proud of, one invoice, no long contract | P4 |

We do not target bargain hunters or capsule-machine users.

## Brand pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Proof point |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Fresh | Coffee is roasted to order, not stored | Shipped no more than 5 days after roasting; roast date printed on every bag |
| Traceable | The customer knows the farm, not just the country | Every bag names the farm or cooperative; 14 origins bought directly or through one importer |
| Flexible | No lock-in for anyone | Subscriptions and office plans can be paused or cancelled monthly |
| Approachable | Specialty coffee without the snobbery | Three taste profiles (fruity, balanced, dark) instead of jargon |

## How we differ from competitors

| Competitor | Their position | Our answer |
|------------|----------------|------------|
| Röstwerk | Cheapest, widest range | We do not compete on price. We compete on freshness and traceability, where their reviews are weakest. |
| Kaffeekiste | The subscription and gifting brand | We match the subscription experience and add what they lack: a single-origin shop and delivery to Austria and Switzerland. |
| Alpenröst | Premium local roaster in Austria and Switzerland | Similar quality at a lower price, with a stronger online experience. |
| BüroBohne | Full-service office coffee with machine rental | Better coffee and monthly cancellation instead of a 12-month contract. |

## Pricing principles

1. Price sits between the mass market and the premium roasters: above Röstwerk and Kaffeekiste, below Alpenröst.
2. Discounts are limited to 20% and are never the main message of a campaign.
3. Preferred incentives are added value: a free sample bag, free shipping, or a brew guide.
4. Brew gear is a service to coffee customers, not a profit centre. We do not try to win on gear prices.

## Tone of voice

- **Warm and plain.** Write as a knowledgeable friend, not a barista champion.
- **Specific.** Name the farm, the roast date, the altitude. Avoid "premium", "finest" and "exclusive".
- **Honest.** Say what a coffee tastes like, including who will not like it.
- **Language.** German first, informal "du". English for Switzerland-wide campaigns only.

## What the brand does and does not do

| We do | We do not |
|-------|-----------|
| Show farmers, roasting and real customers | Use stock photos of latte art |
| Explain brewing in simple steps | Publish content that assumes expert knowledge |
| Partner with small creators who already drink our coffee | Pay celebrities or run TV ads |
| Promote flexibility as a benefit | Use countdown timers, fake scarcity or cancellation hurdles |
| Sell third-party gear we have tested ourselves | Launch own-brand machines or capsules |

## Strategic priorities for 2026

1. Become the first choice for specialty coffee subscriptions in Germany among home enthusiasts.
2. Grow recurring revenue (P1 and P4) to 75% of total revenue, from 63% today.
3. Build awareness in Austria and Switzerland, where Kaffeekiste does not deliver.
