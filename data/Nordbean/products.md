# Product analysis

> Mock data. Nordbean is a fictional company.

Demand is measured as combined monthly search volume in Germany for the product's tracked queries, plus the 12-month trend of that volume.

## P1. Coffee Subscription

### Description

Freshly roasted beans delivered every 2 or 4 weeks. The customer picks a taste profile (fruity, balanced, dark) and Nordbean rotates the origin each delivery. Roasted no more than 5 days before shipping. Pause or cancel at any time.

### Demand

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly search volume | 20,300 |
| 12-month trend | +6% |
| Seasonality | Peak in November – December (gifts), +80% for "kaffee abo geschenk" |
| Price sensitivity | High: 34% churn after the discounted first delivery |
| Customer rating | 4.7 / 5 (1,840 reviews) |

## P2. Single-Origin Beans

### Description

One-off purchases of beans from a single farm or cooperative, in 250 g and 1 kg bags. 14 origins in the range, with one limited edition per month. Each bag shows the roast date and farm.

### Demand

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly search volume | 25,100 |
| 12-month trend | +3% |
| Seasonality | Flat |
| Price sensitivity | Medium: buyers compare with Röstwerk, which is about 25% cheaper |
| Customer rating | 4.8 / 5 (960 reviews) |

## P3. Brew Gear

## P4. Office Coffee Plan

### Description

Monthly bean delivery for offices of 10–100 people, sized by headcount. Includes a free grinder on loan and a dedicated contact person. No machine rental. Cancel monthly.

### Demand

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly search volume | 6,700 |
| 12-month trend | +24% |
| Seasonality | Dips in August and late December |
| Price sensitivity | Low: buyers decide on service and flexibility |
| Customer rating | 4.9 / 5 (31 reviews) |
| Common lost-deal reason | "We need a machine as well" (41% of lost leads) |
